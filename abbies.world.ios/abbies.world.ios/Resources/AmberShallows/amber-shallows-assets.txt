Moss 01 by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/moss_01
CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license
Downloaded 1k glTF, binary, diffuse, normal, ARM and opacity maps.
The moss uses textured cutout mesh fronds, instanced in 3D.

Water bear: original joined Blender mesh, saved as assets/water-bear.blend; exported as indexed geometry.
Other stems, vesicles, claws, internal bodies and suspended motes: procedural geometry.
Three.js r180: vendor/LICENSE.

No generated background image is loaded by the current scene.
Depth of field and bloom are screen-space approximations. Transparent layers share limitations of a single depth buffer. Water lighting is an approximate 16-step unshadowed volume integration, not full fluid or light-transport simulation. The creature membrane combines transparent surface shading with view-dependent edge opacity; it is an artistic approximation, not physically complete tissue refraction.
Temperature and scale indicators are illustrative. No ecological simulation yet.
