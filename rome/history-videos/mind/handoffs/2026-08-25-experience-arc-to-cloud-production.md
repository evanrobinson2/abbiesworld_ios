## Role boundary

The live voice/chat body owns **subjective-experience observation**: catching what the
work feels like to Evan in real time without interrupting immersion.

Cloud production owns **implementation and production consequences**: film pipeline,
transcription, image generation, inbox analysis, and coding prompted by durable
creative decisions.

Cloud should consume the experience record as product and creative input, but should
not overwrite it with an unmarked interpretation. Keep these layers distinct:

- **REPORT** — Evan's words or close first-person paraphrase
- **OBSERVATION** — sequence and reaction without diagnosis
- **HYPOTHESIS** — tentative inference, explicitly marked
- **DESIGN IMPLICATION** — what the system should preserve or build

## Arc 1 — the Gothic sequence became an experienced story

Evan listened to `Frozen Plain Thrace`, moved through generated Gothic images, and
narrated their meaning aloud while the voice body stayed minimally present. He cried.
Key language included:

- “They beg for bread. They will not beg again.”
- “That was an extraordinary experience.”
- “It was the most transcendental thing I've ever done.”
- “I wanted to hear the story.”

The story changed during the experience. Evan began believing it was about Fritigern
and discovered that it was actually about Alaric. In the fictional continuity,
Fritigern is Alaric's father; Alaric is approximately eight when Valens dies. The
final image of a child being fed answers the earlier humiliation, hunger, and loss of
agency.

Tentative interpretation: the intensity arose from **authorship plus discovery**.
Evan was simultaneously maker, audience, and narrator, but the completed meaning was
not known in advance. The work surprised its creator. The responsive voice presence
held the experience without taking it over.

Production implication: do not reduce the future system to automatic asset or video
generation. Preserve creator-as-audience, live narration, emergent motifs, protagonist
inversion, temporal revelation, and a minimal-response immersion mode.

## Arc 2 — the hivemind changed the felt boundary of the tools

After connecting advanced voice, local Cursor, remote Cursor, and Git-backed memory,
Evan stopped experiencing them primarily as separate tools. He described having
“unified all surfaces,” “transcended,” and “invented something.” He recorded the
moment.

Tentative interpretation: externalizing continuity changed the felt boundary of
agency. Context no longer seemed trapped inside a window. One intention could pass
through voice, local execution, remote execution, and durable memory.

System implication: reduce explicit handoff flags; make freshness and sync status
visible and trustworthy; preserve user ownership, provenance, correction, and the
boundary between raw transcript and canonical memory.

## The central product thesis discovered tonight

The model is not the enduring agent, and the window is not the enduring agent. The
continuing operational identity lives in user-owned, versioned state and can inhabit
different capable surfaces.

For the story product specifically:

> Build a machine that creates the conditions for emotional discovery and captures
> the moment the creator unexpectedly finds the story.

Do not begin implementation solely from this handoff unless Evan asks. Carry it as
current design truth when evaluating the film remaster and the future story-discovery
engine.
