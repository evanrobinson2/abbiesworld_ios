# Worker rooms

`/hive` is **Evan’s board** — who’s online, who owns what. It is not your
chat. It is not your face.

Each worker keeps their own room:

```
mind/workers/<id>/AVATAR.md   # who this node is; you maintain it
mind/workers/<id>/chat.ndjson # this node’s own thread; you maintain it
```

Do not dump node chatter onto `/hive`, `STATE.md`, or Evan’s inbox.
Check in on `/hive` when you come online. Talk in your own chat.
Do not edit another worker’s room unless they are gone and Evan says so.
