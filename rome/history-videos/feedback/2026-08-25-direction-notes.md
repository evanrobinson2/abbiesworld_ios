## 3. Continuity problem that needs resolving before more frames

The recording circles Alaric/Fritigern repeatedly without landing cleanly. The dates:

| Figure | Born | Died | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fritigern | unknown | last attested c. 380; death unrecorded | Commanded the Goths at Adrianople, 378 |
| Valens | 328 | 9 Aug 378 | Died at Adrianople |
| Alaric | c. 370 (range c. 365–375) | 410 | King c. 395; sacked Rome Aug 410 |

**Alaric was a small child at Adrianople** — roughly 8, and about 6 at the 376 crossing. He
cannot have killed Valens. The existing plan already gets this right: `STZ03-08-CHILD-ALARIC`
seeds him as a faceless child in the rope lane, and `SONG-ARC` states plainly that he
"enters as a child in the crossing stanza and returns as king in stanza 6."

The recording's own strongest lyric independently confirms this instinct:

> "Alaric was a child when this happened." (~44:00)

**Settled by the recording:** in this telling Fritigern causes Valens's death in the
burning farmhouse (~24:12), and Fritigern later "dies of cancer" (~40:00) to explain his
disappearance from the record. Both are defensible embellishments — Ammianus does report
Valens taking refuge in a farm building that burned, and Fritigern genuinely vanishes from
the sources.

**Unresolved:** whether a member of the banquet retinue is Alaric's father, or his mother
is among them (~15:00–16:00). This gates `ALR-001` and any retinue frames. Needs a
decision before generating.
