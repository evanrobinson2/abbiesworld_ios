# [Sequence name] — Beat Board

Rough frames, in order, with durations. We edit the piece here, before spending
anything on finished imagery.

Density guide: roughly 4–8 frames per minute of finished runtime for still-image
narration with moves. A 90-second pilot is therefore roughly 6–12 frames. Resist
the urge to make it more — long holds with a slow move are the medium's strength.
