## Purpose

Every claim the images make is one of three things: attested, inferred, or invented.
This ledger records which, and it drives the rendering register (see
`02-style-bible.md`). It is the mechanism that makes the visual grammar honest
rather than decorative.

A frame may not be generated until its content has an entry here.

## Register mapping

| Ledger class | Meaning | Register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Attested** | A source states it | R1 Realised |
| **I — Inferred** | Not stated, but follows from evidence or material culture | R1 or R2, case by case |
| **P — Plausible** | Consistent with the period, undocumented for this event | R2 Reconstructed |
| **U — Unknown** | Record silent, contradictory, or the moment unwitnessed | R3 Unrecoverable |

## Rules

1. **Faces of named historical individuals are always invention.** We choose a
   plausible face and then hold it. We never imply the likeness is attested.
2. **Invention must be consistent.** Inventing is permitted; drifting is not.
3. **Where sources conflict, R3.** Do not quietly pick the more cinematic version.
4. **The narration must never claim more than the ledger supports**, and the image
   must never claim more than the narration. Register is the mechanism.
