# Moods

Status: **Step 1 complete — emotion list drafted. Step 2 (mood presets) not started.**

The base aesthetic is fixed (`02-style-bible.md`): cut paper, silhouette figures,
four colours, light as flat shape. Moods are how that single style produces
emotional range without becoming a different style.

---

## Step 1 — The emotional spine

Fifteen states, grouped into six families. Every frame in the finished work should
be assignable to exactly one.

### A. Pressure — before Rome

| # | Mood | Whose | Where in the arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Dread** | Gothic population | Hunnic rumour; a threat never clearly seen |
| 2 | **Uprooting** | Gothic families | The decision to abandon home |

### B. Supplication — the Danube

| # | Mood | Whose | Where in the arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | **Petition** | Fritigern, Alavivus | Asking Rome from a position of weakness |
| 4 | **Endurance** | Gothic population | Waiting. Weather. Time passing badly |
| 5 | **Humiliation** | Gothic population | Being counted, priced, processed |
| 6 | **Hunger** | Gothic families | Famine as physical fact, not metaphor |

### C. Life — threaded throughout

Principle 4. These are not filler. Without them the catastrophe has nothing to
land against, and misery becomes wallpaper.

| # | Mood | Whose | Where in the arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | **Tenderness** | Anyone | Care between people; the bread |
| 8 | **Levity** | Anyone, both peoples | Jokes, play, boredom, appetite, sleep |

### D. The break — Marcianople

| # | Mood | Whose | Where in the arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | **Unease** | Everyone | Something is wrong and nobody knows what |
| 10 | **Betrayal** | Fritigern | The moment it turns |
| 11 | **Fury** | Gothic warriors | Violence as release — and its cost |

### E. Roman interior

Kept as its own family deliberately. Principle 5: these are not villain moods.

| # | Mood | Whose | Where in the arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | **Procedure** | Lupicinus | Administrative coldness; a problem to be managed |
| 13 | **Hubris** | Valens | Imperial confidence; rivalry with Gratian; impatience |

### F. Adrianople

| # | Mood | Whose | Where in the arc |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 | **Terror** | Both armies | Heat, thirst, encirclement, noise, no information |
| 15 | **Ruin** | Nobody | Stillness afterward; the emperor unaccounted for |

### Notes on the list

- **Fury and Terror are different moods and must look different.** Fury is chosen;
  Terror is suffered. Collapsing them would flatten the second half of the film.
- **Humiliation is the load-bearing one.** It is the causal hinge of the whole
  story, and it is the hardest to draw. If the aesthetic cannot do humiliation, the
  audience will not believe the revolt.
- **Procedure and Hubris must not be drawn as villainy.** They are ordinary
  institutional states. That restraint is what makes the outcome disturbing.
- **Dread has no visible antagonist.** The Huns should probably never be shown
  clearly. Dread is an absence mood.

## Step 2 — Mood presets (NOT STARTED)

The base style holds constant. A mood is a fixed preset of the variables that remain
free. To be defined per mood:

| Variable | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Palette weighting | which of the four dominates; how much gold (if any) |
| Light shape | wide soft wedge / narrow hard shard / diffuse / none |
| Layer count | many (space, air, calm) vs few (compression, threat) |
| Edge quality | clean cut vs torn deckle |
| Shape language | curved and rounded vs angular and shard-like |
| Horizon | high (weight, entrapment) vs low (exposure, sky) |
| Symmetry | broken by default; centred only when ceremony is earned |
| Figure density | isolated silhouette vs mass vs crowd-as-band |
| Negative space | generous vs crowded |

Once each mood has a preset, a frame card names a mood and inherits all nine
settings — the same way it inherits a location or a costume.

## Step 3 — Mood proofs (NOT STARTED)

One test image per mood, same aesthetic, to verify the presets actually read as
distinct emotions rather than as the same picture in different colours.
