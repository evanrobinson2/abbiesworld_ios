## Tempo and feel

- **~90–95 BPM**, mid-tempo
- Slight lo-fi swing on hats
- Kick punchy; snare dry and crisp
- Vocals **on-grid**, syncopated against bass — clear consonants, not floating behind the beat

---

## Production signature

- Vocals very **dry and upfront** (little reverb)
- Bass and kick **tight and separated**
- Overall: modern indie, in-the-room, not cathedral, not lo-fi muddy
