# Faction Silhouette Guide — Gothic vs Roman

Status: **draft** — for banquet treachery and all mixed scenes.

In the cut-paper register, **both peoples get identical cut quality, detail budget,
and dignity** (Principle 5). Differentiation is **shape grammar** — never
darker/heavier hand for Romans, never "squalor" shapes for Goths.

**Named principals have visible stylized faces** (cut-paper eyes, nose, mouth) on
every approved turnaround. Crowds and extras remain faceless silhouettes.

---

## The rule at a glance

| Signal | Gothic | Roman |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Leg line** | Trousers/bracae = tapered billow below knee | Columnar tunic to mid-calf; bare calf or sandal strap |
| **Shoulder mass** | Wide from layered cloak + fur deckle edge | Narrower; cloak (paludamentum) pinned one shoulder, falls flat behind |
| **Hair mass** | Long hair silhouette, often to shoulders | Short crop or bald dome |
| **Belt line** | Wide leather belt, hanging pouch, knife | Military cingulum, short sword right hip |
| **Head profile** | Longer skull-hair outline, optional beard mass | Short skull, clean jaw or trimmed beard wedge |
| **Seated at banquet** | Bench, horn or bowl, cloak pooled beside | Chair with back, cup, paludamentum draped over seat back |

**Never use colour to mark faction.** Palette stays indigo / bone / iron grey / gold
for everyone. A gold cup is light, not Roman wealth.

## Banquet treachery — who reads as what

The audience must instantly know **who invited whom** and **who is trapped** without
faces or villain lighting.

### Inside the hall (leaders)

| Figure | Read as | Silhouette keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fritigern | Gothic guest | Tallest; full cloak with fur deckle shoulders; long hair; sword at left hip; seated forward on bench |
| Alavivus | Gothic guest | One-shoulder cloak drape (triangle); fuller beard point; narrower; hands on table |
| Lupicinus | Roman host | Short hair; paludamentum behind chair; tunic column; convivial open-hand gesture — **not** hunched or clawed |

### Outside the hall (retainers)

| Figure | Read as | Silhouette keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gothic retainers | Waiting allies | Trousers, long hair, spears vertical, clustered |
| Roman garrison | Occupiers | Helm crest shapes, oval shields at rest, shorter hair mass, spaced at intervals |

### The treachery beat

When violence breaks, **costume state** changes before character identity:
- Gothic retainers **stand** (height rises, spears angle forward)
- Roman messenger **runs** (paludamentum or cloak streaming, calcei stride)
- Lupicinus stays **seated** one beat longer than he should — ordinary man, wrong moment

---

## Matched-pair test

Before approving any banquet frame, place one Gothic and one Roman principal side by
side at equal scale. Same paper texture, same shadow depth, same detail count. If
one reads as "hero" and one as "villain" by shape alone, redesign.
