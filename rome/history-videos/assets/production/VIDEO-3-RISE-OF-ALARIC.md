## The discovery: your ending is already shot, and so is its rhyme

Two frames already in the manifest, both `review: pass`, both on the CDN:

| ID | Shot | Mood | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `SCENE-06-HUNGER-01` | 17 | 6 Hunger | Roman soldier **withholding** bread loaf from reaching Gothic hands |
| `SCENE-07-TENDERNESS-01` | 21 | 7 Tenderness | Mother **breaking** small bread for child inside wagon shelter |

Same object. Same gesture — hands reaching for bread. Opposite meaning. One is bread denied
by an armed stranger; the other is bread given by a mother, by her own hand.

That is the entire thesis of the trilogy, and it is already drawn. *"They begged for bread.
They will not beg again"* is the caption for these two frames in sequence.

**They are currently four shots apart.** Shot 17 and shot 21, both inside the Danube
humiliation run. That spends the ending in act two.

### Recommendation 1 — pull `SCENE-07-TENDERNESS-01` out of the flight sequence

Reserve it for the final frame of Video 3. It is too strong to use early, and using it twice
halves it. Its current mood-7 slot in "C. Flight and petition" should be filled with a
different Tenderness beat — Principle 4 wants someone still laughing on the road, which is a
different image and cheaper to generate than this one is to replace.

If that frame stays where it is, the trilogy has no ending left to arrive at.

## Recommendation 2 — the Notarius finally writes the correction

`NARRATOR-NOTARIUS.md` left one thing open: the clerk would be very old or dead by 410.

Resolve it by making the whole trilogy his lifelong act of amending a document.

- **Video 1 cold open:** *"I did not write down what the boy saw. That is the part I got
  wrong."* A confession of failure, thirty years late.
- **Video 3 close:** he writes it. And what he finally records is not a battle, a treaty, or
  a sack. It is a woman feeding her son inside a wagon.

The last image stops being only emotional resolution and becomes **the corrected record**. A
man who spent his career counting and pricing people ends by writing down the one thing worth
writing down. The film is the amendment.

This also solves the aging problem without contrivance: he is an old clerk with a thirty-year
grudge against his own tablet.

## The closing child is not Alaric

Worth stating so nobody generates it wrong. Alaric dies in 410, months after the sack. The
child in the final frame is **anonymous** — a generation later, or simply someone's child. The
meaning in `STORY_ARC.md` is explicit: *the people who were forced to beg for bread can feed
their children by their own hands again.*

So the faceless treatment in the existing frame is **correct**, not a shortcut. Per the style
bible, named principals get visible cut-paper faces and everyone else is silhouette. This
mother and child are deliberately anyone.

Optional refinement: if her silhouette language echoes Alaric's mother from Video 1, you get a
generational rhyme without asserting a relationship. Same cut, different woman, forty years on.
