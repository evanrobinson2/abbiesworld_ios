## Lupicinus — hubris, not foolishness

**Settled:** Lupicinus is **not** a fool. He is a competent Roman commander who suffers
from **institutional hubris** — the assumption that starving, disarmed Gothic supplicants
are manageable, and that Roman procedure wins.

**Rejected beat (weak drama, wrong psychology):**

> Lupicinus releases Fritigern → follows him → tries to decimate the Gothic encampment.

No competent commander lets the enemy war-leader walk out so he can march on the camp.
That reads as stupidity, not tragedy.

**Evan's version (stronger):**

| What Lupicinus believes | What is actually true |
| --- | --- |
| He **holds** Fritigern at the banquet — guest, hostage, leverage | Fritigern **chose** to be there; the room is part of the operation |
| The camp is helpless — disarmed, hungry, divided | The camp is **prepared** — signals, laager, soldiers not beggars |
| A gate scuffle proves Gothic disorder; execute the retinue; crisis contained | The scuffle is **staged**; Roman response is **predicted** |
| He can send men to the encampment **while Fritigern remains under guard** | While Lupicinus watches **his** prisoner, the real reversal runs **elsewhere** |
| Romans decimate Goths | Goths decimate Romans (**series cover**) |

Hubris = **misreading who is trapped**. The Caesar figure is the one who looks surprised
(transcript ~27:48).

Principle 5 still holds: Lupicinus is **genuinely convivial** before the turn — not rendered
as a villain caricature (`SONG-ARC` stanza 4).

## Tactical spine (Evan 2026-08-26 + transcript)

1. **River crossing** — see Roman order across the water; supplicants, not invaders.
2. **Danube processing** — institutional humiliation: corrals, disarmament, hostages,
   priced food (`SCENE-05-*`). Child Alaric in the rope lane (seed).
3. **Marcianople invitation** — warmth performs real; Lupicinus reads control.
4. **Gate distraction** — Goths stage scuffle (transcript: *They staged it. They staged
   the scuffle.* ~14:06).
5. **Roman overreach** — Lupicinus orders murder of Gothic men (his hubris: procedure
   solves disorder).
6. **Reversal at the gate** — in Evan's telling the guards **don't** get the clean kill;
   Gothic side completes the reversal (transcript: *in my version, the guards didn't
   slay them. Listen to my story.* ~15:51).
7. **Fritigern stays** — he does **not** leave the banquet side. He remains with
   Lupicinus and his men while the operation runs elsewhere. This is why Lupicinus
   never "allowed Fritigern to leave only to follow him."
8. **Camp operation** — prepared Goths: uniform swap, escape through Roman confusion,
   burn bodies (cover the ruse).
9. **Wagon circle** — link up; laager battle; Roman force broken (**stanza 5**, series
   cover register).
10. **Reveal (Fritigern voice-over or direct address)** — *You thought we were fools.
    Dogs to be kept at heel. We laid a trap for you.* (Transcript spine: *you thought
    they were beggars* ~31:49; *grand strategy all along* ~31:06.)
11. **Why (Alaric child POV)** — verbatim block below; bridge to E03.

Training flashbacks may **interrupt** beats 8–10 (transcript ~32:07): prove soldier
discipline, not desperation.
