# Rome

`history-videos/` is the Gothic Invasion of Rome video drama (the Danube crossing in 376 CE to Adrianople in 378 CE),
imported from `evanrobinson2/rome_history_videos` with its git history. It holds the story bible, character and scene
registry, Midjourney and image prompts, research notes, the frame review app and the pipeline scripts. It sits next to
the Fritigern story (`docs/oracle/BACKLOG.md`) and the Roman kit (`tools/roman-kit/`). Large media is listed in
`history-videos/MEDIA.md` and stays in the source repository.
