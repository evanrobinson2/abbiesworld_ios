# Family Teleport

Recorded 2026-10-22. Evan's ask: get the kids in a room, take a picture of the family, have
the builder make a toy model of each of us, "teleport" us into the holodeck, and build the
world live around us. Make it an adventure through five places.

Page: `/teleport` on the Ranch Atelier site (`dist/teleport/`). Add `?demo=1` to skip the
setup and use sample answers, and `&world=<id>` to start in a given world.

## Run of show

| Beat | What happens | Who drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crew | The family appears as toy characters. Stand-ins until a photo is used. | Evan |
| Madlibs | Seven prompts (a pet, a colour, a food, a ride, treasure, a silly creature, a magic word). Each answer shows whether the builder already knows it or will make a new model. | Kids |
| Builder | "53 models ready in the kit, 1 new." The model order can be downloaded as JSON for the modeler. | Evan |
| Teleport | White flash, beams of light, the family materialises on a teleport pad. The world grows outward from them as blockouts, then sharpens to full detail in a sweep. | Automatic |
| World 1, Sky Meadow | **Watch-only.** Letterbox bars, no buttons, a slow cinematic camera and narration. The kids' animal grazes next to them. It looks like a film on purpose: this is the "that's just Photoshop" moment. "Next" glows when it ends. | Nobody |
| World 2, Dino Valley | **Live.** The letterbox lifts, a red LIVE badge and fps counter appear, and a command bar slides up. Kids say "make it night", "make the T-Rex giant", "T-Rex chase us" and every change lands in a frame or two, with a ⚡ reaction time in milliseconds. Tap a family member, then tap the ground to walk them there. | Kids |
| Secret | Evan presses Next. The map glitches and a sixth stop that was never on the map arrives. | Automatic |
| Abbie's World | Built entirely out of their madlib answers, tinted their favourite colour. | Kids |
| Worlds 3 to 5 | Coral Reef, Moon Base (low gravity, Earth in the sky), Cloud Castle (the finale: cake, presents, fireworks). Saying the magic word anywhere sets off fireworks, a rainbow and a dance. | Kids |

Presenter keys: `N` next world, `M` map, `R` remix ("switch it up"), `B` builder panel,
`1` to `6` jump to a world, `Space` push-to-talk in live worlds.

## How it is built

| Piece | File | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model kit | `dist/teleport/kit.mjs` | 178 procedural models (animals, dinosaurs, food, vehicles, places, props) with named parts and three detail levels. Matches madlib words, plurals, colours and sizes ("giant purple dragon"). |
| Kit manifest | `dist/teleport/kit-manifest.json` | Generated by `tools/family-teleport/kit-manifest.mjs`: parts, triangle budget per level, matching words, which worlds use each model. The script also runs the self-checks. |
| Family | `dist/teleport/family.mjs` | Toy characters from a small character sheet (colours, hair style, height, glasses), walk cycle and emotes, the on-device photo colour picker. |
| Worlds | `dist/teleport/worlds.mjs` | The five stops and the secret one as recipes (sky, ground, particles, placements, madlib slots, narration), the remix styles and the model order. |
| Commands | `dist/teleport/commands.mjs` | Kid-speak to world actions, on the device, with no network and no model tokens. |
| Page | `dist/teleport/app.mjs`, `index.html`, `teleport.css` | Scene, flow, show camera, voice (Web Speech API), taps, builder panel. |
| Creator hook | `dist/simulations/holodeck/stage.mjs` | With `?creator=<room>` the ship's computer (Claude, see `HOLODECK.md`) drives this page too. The stage now builds kit ids locally, so `spawn {asset:"dragon"}` works here with no download. |

## Token and bandwidth budget

- The whole adventure (56 distinct models, about 500 placed things) is code: 0 KB of model
  downloads and 0 model tokens. The kit grows by adding recipes, not files.
- Every world appears as a blockout first (about 25k triangles for the whole kit) and then
  upgrades to full detail (about 290k) as the family arrives. The builder panel (`B`) shows the
  live numbers.
- Live commands are parsed on the device. Only what the kit cannot match becomes a model
  order (a labelled stand-in appears at once), which is where the modeler spends tokens:
  low detail first, upgraded when the story reaches it, cheapest model (Haiku) for bulk.
- Claude is only needed for open-ended direction, through the Creator hook.

## Privacy

The kids' photos and likenesses never leave the device in this version. The photo picker
reads colours from a local canvas and clears it when it closes; the character sheets and
madlib answers live only in the browser's local storage. Nothing about the family is
uploaded, logged, committed or put in model storage. The real-likeness pipeline in
`tools/family-teleport/README.md` keeps photos and sheets in a git-ignored private folder.
