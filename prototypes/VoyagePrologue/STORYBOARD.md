# Let’s rescue our friends

Narrative and blocking scaffold. Timings are provisional (approximately 47 seconds, including a held handoff). No new character canon. Net staging and short dialogue are creative proposals, not approved asset designs.

## Visual rules

- Every image must pass identity, emotion, action, tone, color/light, silhouette, framing, eyeline and adjacent-shot continuity review. Record source and approval separately from suitability.
- Missing story art stays visibly labeled as missing in the director scaffold; reference portraits never stand in for narrative action.
- Effects must have a source or function: petals from the canopy, shadow from bullies, panel cuts for cause and reaction.
- No giant marble, escaped-marble subplot, abstract captions, unrelated name cards, or decorative symbolism.
- Review silent: can a child tell who was happy, what the bullies did, and why Abbie follows?

## 1. An afternoon together — 7 seconds

Fox lines up a shot. Bramble leans in, then looks at Fox with a friendly challenge.

**Player understands:** These friends enjoy being together.
**Emotion:** Warm, safe, playful

**Every planned visible element:**

- Wide view of one clearing beneath the flowering tree; establishes the place we will return to.
- Fox at left and Bramble at right, full bodies and readable faces; establishes the existing friends.
- A small scratched play circle and a few ordinary-sized marbles between them; explains their activity without becoming the subject.
- A few petals falling from the visible flowering canopy; connects motion to the setting.
- Soft afternoon light and a dark teal root framing the pair; keeps faces readable.

**Text:** None.
**Sound:** Leaf rustle, small movement sounds, a light playful musical phrase.
**Motion:** Slow lateral drift of no more than 2%; sparse petals. No zoom into a marble.
**Transition:** A reaction panel opens from the side toward which Fox looks.

**Art needed:**

- New wide action plate with BOTH approved character designs, relaxed posture, matching eyelines and full paws.

**Compatibility finding:** Composition and palette reference only. Existing image reads as concentration; it lacks clear shared enjoyment and Bramble’s clover details.

## 2. A little friendly teasing — 7 seconds

Fox takes a shot and misses. Fox makes a sheepish face. Bramble laughs kindly; Fox joins in.

**Player understands:** They are having fun with each other, not merely staring at objects.
**Emotion:** Amusement, affection

**Every planned visible element:**

- The same clearing remains behind the action to preserve location.
- Medium shot of Fox making the shot; the paw belongs visibly to Fox, with the face still readable.
- One narrow insert of the missed shot, less than a second, only if needed to explain the reaction.
- Bramble’s laughing reaction panel followed by Fox’s sheepish smile; supplies cause and effect.
- Consistent marbles, tree roots, screen positions and light; no unexplained scene change.

**Text:** None. Expressions carry this beat.
**Sound:** One quiet marble click, a short shared laugh; music continues.
**Motion:** Two panels enter in sequence, following shot → miss → reaction. Hold the expressions long enough to read.
**Transition:** The laughter cuts off when Bramble looks beyond Fox.

**Art needed:**

- Fox shot pose and sheepish reaction.
- Bramble laughing reaction with matching gaze and recognizable clover ornaments.

**Compatibility finding:** Current paw insert is too dominant and does not show a miss. Do not use it as proof of this action.

## 3. Someone blocks the light — 4 seconds

Bramble stops laughing and looks up. Fox follows that gaze.

**Player understands:** Someone unwelcome has arrived.
**Emotion:** Surprise, unease; not horror

**Every planned visible element:**

- Same medium two-shot with both friends; their changed expressions are the subject.
- A directional shadow crosses the play circle from the entrance side; it is cast by the approaching bullies.
- Petals continue moving normally; the world does not magically freeze.
- A restrained close-up of Bramble looking up; establishes the next cut’s eyeline.

**Text:** None.
**Sound:** Music pauses; footsteps enter from the shadow side.
**Motion:** Shadow crosses once. Camera settles. No blackout, screen shake, or ominous particle change.
**Transition:** Eyeline cut to the approaching bullies.

**Art needed:**

- Surprised but unharmed Fox/Bramble reactions.
- Shadow layer matching the bully silhouettes and scene lighting.

**Compatibility finding:** Missing. Smiling or concentrated portraits cannot substitute for surprised reactions.

## 4. The bullies spoil the game — 6 seconds

Raze steps between the friends and scuffs the play circle. Vix blocks the way out. The friends draw together.

**Player understands:** These bullies deliberately interrupt the friends’ fun.
**Emotion:** Rude, unfair, mildly threatening

**Every planned visible element:**

- Raze enters from the established shadow side in the same clearing.
- Raze’s foot disturbs the ring and moves a few marbles a short distance; shows the interruption without a hero-marble shot.
- Vix is visible behind, blocking the path; makes the group threat legible.
- Fox and Bramble together on the opposite side; preserves geography and their friendship.
- One angled panel edge breaks the previously calm layout as Raze intrudes; visual emphasis belongs to his action.

**Text:** Optional short line from Raze: “Game’s over.” No name card or narrator.
**Sound:** Footstep, scuff, one short low musical accent.
**Motion:** Raze crosses the panel boundary once; avoid repeated shakes or flashing.
**Transition:** Cut on Vix moving toward the friends; do not skip the capture action.

**Art needed:**

- Raze full-body intrusion/scuff pose.
- Vix blocking pose.
- Friends’ wary two-shot.

**Compatibility finding:** Existing Raze portrait is an identity reference only. Folded arms do not communicate the scuff or capture.

## 5. The friends are taken — 7 seconds

Vix drops a loose rope net around both friends. Raze and Vix lead them up the path. Bramble looks back.

**Player understands:** The friends have been captured and are being taken up the path.
**Emotion:** Unfairness, concern; clearly safe to rescue

**Every planned visible element:**

- Panel one: net visibly descends around the friends; establishes how capture happens.
- Panel two: both friends visible and unharmed inside roomy mesh, faces unobstructed; avoid constriction, pain or violent struggle.
- Panel three: the same bullies and captured friends move uphill, in the same screen direction.
- The play area is left behind in the lower foreground; links the loss to the fun we just saw.

**Text:** Bramble calls “Abbie!” once. No explanatory caption.
**Sound:** Soft rope movement, footsteps, the call; restrained music.
**Motion:** Three deliberate cuts: capture → readable result → departure. No impact flash or comic violence.
**Transition:** The call bridges to Abbie’s arrival at the clearing.

**Art needed:**

- Three matching action plates with net continuity and all four established characters.
- Net is a proposed staging prop, not a new magical power or piece of lore.

**Compatibility finding:** Missing. Do not imply capture using smiling portraits, generic silhouettes, or a cut straight to empty ground.

## 6. Abbie sees what happened — 6 seconds

Abbie and her dog enter the clearing as the call reaches them. She sees her friends being led away, then turns toward the path.

**Player understands:** Abbie knows her friends need help and where they were taken.
**Emotion:** Concern turning to resolve

**Every planned visible element:**

- Abbie and her established unicorn Shih Tzu enter as a pair; exact approved designs required.
- Disturbed play circle sits small in the foreground; continuity clue, not a mystery to solve.
- Over Abbie’s shoulder, the friends and bullies remain visible on the near uphill path; provides direct evidence, not inferred tracking.
- Close-up of Abbie’s face changing from concern to determination.
- Dog looks along the same path beside her; establishes companionship without inventing powers.

**Text:** None.
**Sound:** The distant call, a small concerned dog sound, a hopeful musical phrase begins.
**Motion:** Cut along Abbie’s eyeline, then return to her resolved expression.
**Transition:** Follow Abbie and the dog turning toward the mountain.

**Art needed:**

- Abbie/dog arrival two-shot and Abbie concerned/resolved expressions.
- Verify an approved companion reference before generating; do not improvise its design.
- Over-shoulder plate preserving departure direction.

**Compatibility finding:** Missing. Happy Abbie portrait is not an adequate concerned reaction; companion reference still needs verification.

## 7. There’s the way up — 6 seconds

Abbie and her dog face the path. The camera follows it upward, then settles back at the first playable stretch.

**Player understands:** The rescue route goes up this mountain.
**Emotion:** Determination, inviting adventure

**Every planned visible element:**

- Abbie and dog small at the foot of the climb; provides scale and a clear starting point.
- A continuous winding route climbs through the established mountain environment.
- Cloud layers obscure portions of the higher route naturally; communicates distance without inventing a floating kingdom.
- Return or dissolve to the matching first gameplay location; the mountain view must agree with the actual world.

**Text:** Abbie: “Come on. Let’s get our friends.”
**Sound:** Hopeful phrase rises; wind replaces clearing ambience.
**Motion:** One controlled upward pan, about four seconds. Reduced motion uses three stationary views.
**Transition:** Match the path position and horizon to the game camera.

**Art needed:**

- Reviewed tall climb plate with legible continuous route.
- Matching foreground Abbie/dog layers and first gameplay frame.

**Compatibility finding:** Existing tall climb art must be inspected for route and color continuity before use; not cleared by filename alone.

## 8. The player takes over — 4 seconds

The camera rests at the start. Abbie is ready. The player taps to begin.

**Player understands:** I am helping Abbie rescue her friends.
**Emotion:** Agency, readiness

**Every planned visible element:**

- The real first gameplay view, sharing the preceding path alignment.
- Abbie and dog in their starting positions if supported by the actual game scene.
- One large accessible button: “Let’s rescue them.”
- Normal game controls appear only after the tap; no fake gameplay or unexplained collectibles.

**Text:** Button only: “Let’s rescue them.”
**Sound:** Music continues across the handoff without restarting.
**Motion:** Letterbox edges withdraw gently; input transfers exactly once. Reduced motion cuts directly.
**Transition:** Emit the standalone completion event; production host owns starting gameplay.

**Art needed:**

- Verified first gameplay view and host completion callback.

**Compatibility finding:** Scaffold can emit a completion event, but production game integration remains absent.
