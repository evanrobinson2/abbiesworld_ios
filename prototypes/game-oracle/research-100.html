Oracle · 100-game research library

Recommended research order for Abbie’s World and the mechanics library. Start with ranks 1–10: production, needs, movement and readable simulation.

45 acquired originals plus 55 explicitly named free replacements. No purchases. Replacements broaden the research collection; they are not claimed to reproduce the original games. Study questions are proposed, not gameplay findings. Required runtimes and separate content packs are recorded in the downloadable catalog.

100 / 100 downloaded to Samsung. Package checks recorded; games not installed or play-tested. Status checked 21 October 2026.
