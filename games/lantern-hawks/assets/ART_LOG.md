# Art log (round 5, 2026-10-10)

Every manifest key with real art: where the file came from and the prompt that made it. Model gpt-image-2.5-flare, quality high, 1536x1024 for scenes and 1024x1024 for portraits and pixel pieces.
Raw generations: `proofs/r5/raw/` (earlier tries kept as `*.vN.png`). Prompts: `proofs/r5/jobs.py` builds `jobs.json`; `gen.py` generates (an `edit` job repaints another job's output through the images edits endpoint, which is how each occupied city keeps the day layout); `post.py` writes the files below and merges `manifest.json`.

## Post-processing

- Painterly scenes: cropped to 16:9 (city scenes keep 40 px top and bottom with a 9% vertical squash so no building is cut), 1280x720 JPEG, all under 400 KB. Manifest `fit: [320,180]` makes the registry resample them smoothly to the 320x180 canvas.
- Portraits: 256x256 JPEG, `fit: [64,64]`.
- Pixel pieces (mechs 64x64, figurines 16x24, cards 24x24, port hex): flat grey background cut out (border-connected plus large enclosed pockets), area-downscaled, quantized without dither (32 colours mechs and hex, 24 figurines, 16 cards), hard alpha, 1px dark edge on sprites 32 px and up. `@big` copies (mechs 192, figurines 48x72) are not registered; they are there for a later higher-resolution screen.
- Hex tiles `hex.plains` to `hex.cave` were already in place and are untouched.

## Rejected and regenerated

- `card_laser` v1 read as a glowing sword; v2 drew a whole robot. the final version (a detached laser cannon) kept.
- `fig_squad` v1 put a mecha behind the pilots; regenerated as three pilots only.
- `poi_hall`, `poi_citadel`, `poi_comms`, `poi_lounge` v1 had a mecha standing indoors (the mech rule in the prompt made the model add one); regenerated with people only.
- `proofs/r4/raw/r4-01.png` and `r4-02.png` (approved city proofs) were not used: r4-01's practice field shows blue-and-white mecha with head fins close to a famous franchise, and r4-02 has a different layout from r4-01, so one set of hotspots could not fit both. The new day city and its occupied repaint replace them.
- `proofs/r4/raw/r4-05.png` (figurine sheet) passed the franchise check but had only five of the seven designs and one shared rim colour; each figurine was generated on its own instead so it cuts cleanly.

## Known caveats

- The hospital in the home city art and `scene.poi.hospital` (r4-03) carry a red cross. The red cross is a protected emblem in many countries and some games have been asked to remove it; the Port clinic uses a green cross. Swap if that matters.
- `scene.title` is registered, but the title screen draws its own procedural art and does not read that key yet.
- Figurines are 16x24 because the map draws them at natural size with a fixed offset; they read as tokens, not portraits.
