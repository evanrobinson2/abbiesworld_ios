# App Summary: Abbie's World

Abbie's World is a child-directed exploration and creation game for iPad. Kids farm ingredients, craft creature cards, sell extras for gems, and decorate their treehouse.

The core loop is **farm → craft → sell → decorate**. Home World holds Abbie's and Ani's treehouses, the Card Factory, Card Vault, and Card Shop. The Ingredient Farm sits east of home. Adventure World sits north.

The original Anything Machine (carousel image creation) still lives in the app as the starting screen, with minigames including Creature Lab, Dino Picnic, Waypoint Navigation, Balloon Pop, and Picture Carver. Abbie's World itself launches from Games, or with `-launchAbbiesWorld`.
