# Halloween Media Pack

Childish, spooky, funny, and a little silly. Nothing scary, gory, or mean.

Skin switch: **Normal** (server assets) ↔ **Spooky** (this bundled pack).

Spooky always uses the 4-carousel layout: Monster, Costume, Haunt, Spooky Style.

## Layers

| Layer | What it is | Spooky treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Full-bleed iPad scene behind everything | Candy-night haunted playground with a goofy moon |
| Carve-outs | 4 rounded windows the carousels sit in | Grape-glass windows with orange stitch borders and tiny bats |
| Tile chrome | Frame around each choice | Sticker tile: cream paper, candy-corn corners, orange/black dashed border, name banner |
| Tile art | The picture inside the sticker | 10 monsters, 10 costumes, 10 haunts, 10 styles |
| Music | Bundled spooky playlist | 3 uploaded instrumentals |

## Art rules

- Cute, round, readable at ~180pt carousel size
- No text, letters, numbers, or watermarks on the pictures
- No gore, no real-scare horror, no weapons
- Candy palette: tangerine, grape, lime, bubblegum, cream
- Bold dark-purple outlines
