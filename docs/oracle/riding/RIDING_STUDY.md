# AW-STUDY-0056 Trick Day (riding school tile) — proposal

Evan, 2026-10-23: a riding school tile where you train horses with cards and they do tricks, like dressage but something
fun for kids. Preview: https://app.abbies.world/r2/previews/riding-school/index.html (source `preview/riding-school/`).

Core idea: a horse learns because you are kind, and you can see it learning (positive-reinforcement, clicker-style teaching,
which is our practice-reps loop: attempt, a little better, retry). Trick cards (Bow, Spin, Yes, Wave, Weave, Hop, Sidestep,
Statue) are played onto a sand ring; the first try is wobbly and funny, the card's rarity ladder grows with the horse, and a
well-learned trick is offered by the horse unprompted. Three cards make a routine set to the ring's music. Hoofprints paint the
sand (Paint the World's colour). Dressage is the grown-up Patterns mode: draw a shape, the horse walks it, the paint line is the
score, shown in the workbench view. No rearing, falling, whips or failing; the horse can say "not today".

Open decisions for Evan: horseshoe charm would be an eleventh charm (set is ten). We have horse-idle/feeding/rest models but need a
rigged horse for tricks. Tour: no for now.
