Proofs, not builds. Evan, 2026-10-23 04:44Z: "make a study .. I want to see proofs": Abbie's café in four views, 2-3
options each, in three styles. Nothing is modelled until Evan picks.

- Page: Studies site, `prototypes/ranch-atelier/dist/studies/cafe/` (app.abbies.world/#cafe). One row per view, one
  column per style, four Midjourney options per cell.
- Prompts and palettes: `tools/midjourney/prompts/coffee-shop-0013.json`. Images and job ids:
  `tools/midjourney/outputs/coffee-shop-0013/` (`provenance.json`).

## Views

1. Its own tile: the café alone on a hex tile, as a board-game piece.
2. In a town: the café on the corner of a market-town tile (see AW-STUDY-0006 The City Tile; the café is one of that
   study's hero buildings, "the Morning Café's home").
3. Outside: the storefront at eye level.
4. Inside: the room.

## Palette source

Leatrice Eiseman, *Color: Messages & Meanings, A Pantone Color Resource* (Hand Books Press, Gloucester MA, 2006;
distributed by North Light Books, Cincinnati, Ohio). It is not her earlier *Pantone Guide to Communicating with
Color* (Grafix Press, 2000). The palettes above take their mood families from the book. The hex values are our
reading of those moods, not the book's chip numbers, which we could not check from here: swap in the exact Pantone
chips from Evan's copy when we fix a direction.

## Constant across styles

- The score lives on the café's painted sign (three stars in every prompt): diegetic, in the Ranch dialect of painted
  signs. 
- Abbie pink is in every style; what changes is how grown-up it is.
- PG; no children's faces (people are absent or tiny and distant).
