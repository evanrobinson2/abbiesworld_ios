The City · founding simulation

How the town was founded

Nothing here is written by hand. The land is computed first: hills, two rivers that meet, the sea, drainage, erosion, flood marks and sheltered water. A boat party of thirty picks the safe place that costs the least walking. Then the town grows year by year from food, births, deaths and newcomers; feet wear paths where people walk; and a well, bridge, quay, mill, market, bell tower and school go up when the simulation measures the need. Every line of the story is a fact from this run.
