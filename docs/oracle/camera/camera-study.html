AW-STUDY-0018

Camera and Controls on Open Land

Our camera was made for floating hex tiles. On real, continuous land its pivot floats into the sky, it passes through hills, and one finger spins the world instead of moving you across it. Games on real land keep the pivot on the ground and let you grab the land, and the view tilts toward the horizon as you come down. The Basin now uses a camera built that way.

  1. The pivot is always a point on the ground.
  2. The view tilts from 64° above the horizon high up to 13° near the ground.
  3. The camera rises over ridges quickly and settles back slowly, and never gets closer than 2.5 m to the ground.
  4. At eye level the lake and the edge of the map stop you, and you can wade through rivers.
  5. No gizmos, reticles or labels. The camera adds nothing to the world.

Holding a placed tile still moves the tile. Everywhere else, a drag moves the camera. Once a land world has a player character, the step-in move will land over the character's shoulder (AW-STUDY-0011) instead of at the character's eyes.
