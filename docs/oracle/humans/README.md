# Human characters in 3D games (Oracle study, 2026-10-23)

Evan asked the Oracle for 3D games with human characters and what they teach about making a good 3D human.
`human-ladder.html` is the page (published at https://claude.ai/artifact/5zRH7vVn1aqbTuPeb5n4pZ): 30 games from the
Oracle's 379 profiles, sorted on a five-rung ladder from toy figures to realism, with body proportions (heads tall),
face, hands, detail level and one thing to borrow for each. Written observations from public knowledge; no game art.

Takeaways: commit to one stylisation level for the whole body; heads grow as games get friendlier (2–4 heads tall for
toys, 6–7 heroic, 7.5–8 realistic); silhouette before detail; simple big faces that keep the nose; hands simplified,
never removed; detail lives in big clothing shapes; flat or softly painted colour with a few material roles.
Suggested rung for Abbie's World: 4–6 heads tall, real adult limb proportions, simple face with nose and brows,
mitten hands with a thumb, chunky clothes (between Disney Dreamlight Valley and Valheim). A suggestion, not canon.
