What stands out

  • Your library leans on the world, not the screen. On average 43% of a game's information lives in the world itself, and 30% sits in overlays.
  • Only 32 of 104 keep the HUD within our 8% budget. Kingdom Two Crowns, Mouthwashing, Bongo Cat, Alpha Zoo and Sable are the cleanest. Card games are the heaviest, with Magic Arena and Breachway at about 30%.
  • Feeling-on-the-lens is rare. 35 games use it at all, mostly horror and found footage: Bodycam, Doki Doki, Mouthwashing.
  • "Nothing on screen" is rarer still, and the games that do it are the cozy builders you love: Tiny Glade, Townscaper, Dorfromantik, Kingdom Two Crowns.
  • Your library is mostly strategy and survival (22 and 17 games). Cameras split evenly between top-down, third person, first person, isometric and the diorama orbit.

A living toy world that answers you
