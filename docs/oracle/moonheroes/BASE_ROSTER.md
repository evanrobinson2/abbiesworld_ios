- True shapes: women keep real adult proportions (chest, waist, hips) from the Bodies study canon; men the same.
  Shape follows the canon and the type, never a fantasy exaggeration.

  background, untextured white clay. Faceted Hero planes (AW-STUDY-0029).
- Medium poly: 6,000 to 12,000 faces after cleanup. Every body goes through learn.py so the gear kit
