Motif, aesthetic and palette

What makes a world look like one world, and how its colours tell a child what is happening without a single word. Fourteen games, every Abbie's World number measured from real captures, and a rubric the Game Oracle can run.

The core rule

One signal, one palette

A palette is a function of the world's state: place, time, season, weather and mood. When night falls the world goes dark and the windows come on; the glass refracts the night by itself. The short version

Ten rules

  1. Value before hue. A world reads in greyscale first. Use Squint above: the proposed barn stays a barn.
  2. A palette is roles, not swatches: ground, figure, accent, backdrop, light and shadow.
  3. The saturation ladder. What you touch is the most saturated, the ground quieter, the backdrop quietest.
  4. Accents are scarce and reserved. Under a tenth of the image, one meaning each (Journey's red, A Short Hike's feather).
  5. Warm light, cool shadow. Shadows take the sky's colour; nothing goes neutral grey.
  6. Coherence is a constraint everything obeys: a parts list, a small material set, two colours, a grammar.
  7. A motif is a shape with a meaning that recurs in three or more things.
  8. Shape language is a promise. Round is friendly, square is steady, triangle is alert.
  9. The palette carries state in the world. And never by colour alone: shape, motion or sound too.
  10. The UI speaks the world's palette. Glass, ink and active colour come from the world's own roles.

The paintings are warm. The game is not.

The checker clusters each image in OKLab and measures warmth (share of colour in reds, oranges and yellows). The concept paintings Evan picked for the Ranch rebuild (six shown here) sit at 0.84 to 0.96. The live Ranch sits at 0.03 to 0.05: a cold yellow-green world on a grey void. Seasons do move it (autumn reaches 0.36); the clock does not.

Gate G4 · the light follows the signal

Night is lighter than day

Mean OKLab lightness over the same world crop. The "night" setting is 0.015 lighter than day; "midnight" is 0.03 darker. The rubric asks for at least 0.12. The panel went dark, the world stayed in daylight.

Motif and shape language

Four motifs for the Ranch

A motif recurs in three or more things and means something in play. The teal roof cap is already in every concept painting; the bell is the only brass in the world, so brass means "something to know".
