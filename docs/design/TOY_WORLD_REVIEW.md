## Scope and limits
Title uses actual backpack/charm models. Home uses real Meshy Abbie/cars with study furniture. Design placement, undo and local saving work. Capture previews image/video/audio locally and builds a selected illustrative scene, not an AI reconstruction. No upload, charge or sharing is performed. Home customization is local study state, not production inventory. Charms remain the signature reward; trophies, toy cars and dressing are additional facets.

Racer reuses the fixed-step model with lane changes/launch timing. Five added recipes vary length, turns, grades, pad positions and colors: layout studies, not five finished themed environments. All finish; greedy lane/launch choices improve time over center-lane no-launch runs. Local records/prizes and mock town scores remain labeled. No registry entry is promoted.
