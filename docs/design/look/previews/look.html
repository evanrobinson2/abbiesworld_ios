Design direction · for Evan

Abbie’s World

Every page is a table standing under the world’s own sky. Things sit on it as thick toy pieces you could pick up, buttons press down like painted wooden blocks, and glass is a small lens. When the world turns to night, so does every page.

Pieces

A glass lens
A painted sign

Tiles have a wooden edge and cast a shadow on the table. Blocks press down 5 px. Glass stays small and shows the sky through it.

  • The world is the backdropEvery page sits under the world’s sky, at the world’s time of day.
  • Things, not panelsAnything you touch is a thick tile with a shadow, never a flat box with an outline.
  • Blocks, not linksButtons are painted toy blocks with an icon, and they press down.
  • Glass is a lensSmall and floating. Never a frosted sheet over the world.
  • Show the thingReal art and live 3D on every page, with words kept short.
