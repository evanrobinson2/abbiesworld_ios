Local adaptations for Crayon Physics Release 1:
- Body shape discriminator and circle/box-circle dispatch (FUN_004c7fc8).
- Circle collisions reconstructed from FUN_004c7ab5 and FUN_004c7c7c.
- Normalize zero vector to zero (FUN_004c84d0).
- Joint accumulated impulse multiplied by serialized relaxation (FUN_004ccde8).
- Both serialized local anchors and initial accumulated impulse loaded directly.
- Fixed float timestep 1/60 and source solver iteration count.
- C handle bridge owns bodies/joints and safely removes cached contacts.
- Contact ordering uses monotonically increasing insertion IDs instead of heap
  addresses, making repeated replays independent of allocator reuse. This is an
  intentional portability adaptation, not proof of original contact ordering.

Known fidelity limits: this upstream revision is not recovered original source;
circle contact feature IDs are uncertain in decompilation and use a stable zero;
original contact ordering and float math can differ across platforms. No
bit-for-bit or original executable solution-parity claim is made.
