## Animation Contract Addendum

During walking animation, the following pose-appropriate variations are PERMITTED:
- Hair flowing more dramatically during forward motion
- Arms swinging naturally with stride
- Body lean changing with gait phase
- Clothing folds responding to movement

The following are NOT PERMITTED even during animation:
- Hair color change
- Costume color change
- Face structure change
- Additional accessories appearing
- Different clothing items
- Art style change
