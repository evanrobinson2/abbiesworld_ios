# Cycle 2: Shape and Silhouette Decisions

## Final Canonical Silhouette

File: `canonical-silhouette.png`

## Design Specifications

### Proportions
- **Total Height:** ~100 units
- **Head:** ~30-33 units (1/3 of total)
- **Torso:** ~15-17 units
- **Legs:** ~45-50 units (including boots)
- **Head width:** ~22-25 units

### Hair Geometry
- **Main element:** One large curved fin/triangle shape flowing backward
- **Secondary elements:** 1-2 smaller pointed shapes below main
- **Total distinct hair shapes:** 2-3 (NOT more)
- **Direction:** Flowing backward (leftward in right-facing profile)

### Limb Specifications
- **Arms:** Thin, hanging naturally at sides
- **Arm-torso gap:** Mandatory visible negative space
- **Legs:** Long, thin, slightly shorter than half body height
- **Leg-leg gap:** Mandatory visible negative space
- **Hands:** Small, simplified
- **Feet/boots:** Small, sturdy, simple block shapes

### Accessories
- **Backpack/satchel:** Simple rounded shape on back
- **No other accessories in silhouette**

### Posture
- **Stance:** Relaxed neutral standing
- **Lean:** Slight forward lean (~5-10 degrees)
- **Direction:** Right-facing profile

## Rejected Alternative

V1 had more organic, flowing hair with 4-5 tendrils. This was rejected because:
1. Higher number of elements increases consistency risk
2. Organic shapes harder to reproduce across generations
3. More prone to drift during pose-conditioned generation

- [x] Distinctive without accessories
- [x] Clear limb separation
