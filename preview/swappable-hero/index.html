Swappable hero

Redesign first, rebuild after (Evan, 02:03Z). This page answers "who is she in the overland town today?" and drafts how the player becomes Abbie, Ani, Daddy or anyone in the family with one small tap, sharing the dressing room with the backpack and the buggy. No code has changed.

1. Who she is today

I read the code on main. In the town, on the hex board, I found no player body at all: you look down on the tiles, and the people are townsfolk stand-ins. Abbie only has a body in car mode and in the games.

4. Keeping each person's look

  • One small saved look per person, aw-look-<id>: slot to part, plus colours by material role (hair, top, bottom, shoes). Swapping loads that person's look; nothing is lost by switching back.
  • Colours first, parts later. Today's models are whole baked files, so recolouring by role works now; separate garments on the shared 20 bones come later (the parameterized base in docs/research/).
  • The fitted base layer is always under any clothing. PG, always.
  • Faces are designed once and never redrawn, never made from photos. A swap never touches a face; only the hair, clothes and colours move.
  • Every move works for everyone: all heroes share the 20 bones, and hero-anims.mjs re-fits the 83 clips to each person's height, so a child does not float.

5. What a swap changes (the whole game adapts)

Body

Height and stride change, so the camera height and walking speed follow the person (Daddy sees from higher up than Abbie).

Voice and lines

Each person has a designed voice. Townsfolk greet whoever you are ("Hello, Ani!") through the same quest givers.

Car mode

The rider on the toy car becomes the chosen hero in place of the nine-block Abbie.

The bag

The backpack is worn by whoever you are, in their colours, using the existing chest-bone mount.

7. Choices for Evan (nothing built until you decide)

a. How to swap. Recommended: tap your own bead and pick from the fan (two taps, you can see who you will become). Alternative: one tap cycles to the next person with no picker.
b. What is shared. Recommended: the notebook, quests, charms and backpack contents are shared by the whole family, and only the look, voice and greetings change per person. Alternative: each person has their own bag and notebook.
c. People without a model. Recommended: grey silhouettes until their Meshy model exists, no stand-ins. Alternative: spend the remaining credits on Mommy, Lexi and Jeff first.
d. Who is "Evan"? Recommended: Evan plays as Daddy-now (A3). Alternative: a separate Evan model.
e. Order once approved. (1) Meshy Abbie replaces the nine-block rider in car mode, (2) Abbie walks the town as her real hero, (3) the swap fan with Abbie, Ani and Daddy, (4) colours in the dressing room, (5) garments as parts, (6) the rest of the family as models arrive.
