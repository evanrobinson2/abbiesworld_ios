Oracle consultation15
  • Townscaper: click to place, a town grows itself; no HUD but a colour palette (4% of the screen). The model for "auto first pass, then you"
  • Tiny Glade: gridless building with procedural detail added around what you draw; a tools tray only (8%)
  • Dorfromantik: tile placement with soft, tactile feedback (6%); what a hex edit should feel like
  • Besiege: the machine is the readout and physics is honest; failure is funny (8%). Sets the bar for the Physics bench
  • LEGO Bricktales: build, then a test run shows success in the world (10%)
  • Spore: creature, tribe, city and space editors that reshape themselves per stage; parts show what they do
  • Garry's Mod: spawn menu and tool gun over a physics sandbox (10%); the cost of too many tools
  • Planet Zoo: deep freeform building with welfare panels (20%); the "fiddly" end we avoid by default
  • Cities: Skylines: info views recolour the whole city; a model for the Sky and Physics overlays
  • Astroneer: terrain tool with a backpack that shows items physically (4%)
  • Bad North: a whole battle on a toy island, almost no numbers (6%)
  • Kerbal Space Program: build, launch, learn from physics; precise overlays only where needed (18%)
  • Minecraft: hotbar of blocks and an endless sandbox (9%); the tray of cards is its cousin
  • RimWorld: deep sim parameters behind tabs (22%); what the Lab bench must not become
