Capture: video and sound, drafted

Status: drafted. The cloud path is written and tested with a pretend gateway. The page doesn't send video or sound yet, and nothing is live.

1. On your device
Pick a video. The browser takes up to 6 still pictures and, for sound, a clip up to 30 seconds. The video itself never leaves.
2. Cap check first
Sign-in, then the spending cap is charged. About 7 cents for a whole video with sound, so $1 covers about 13.
3. Safety look
Pictures and words are checked together. A possible child anywhere holds the whole video. Sexual or unsafe is turned away. If the check can't decide, nothing is made.
4. Sound becomes words
The clip is written down as words, those words get the same check, and only the words go on. The clip isn't kept.
5. One themed tile
The planner builds the same tile as for a photo. Nothing is stored.
Not done yet
The picker and sampler on the page. A live check of the speech model (sound answers "not switched on" until it's named). Whether children's voice clips may be sent at all is your call.
